CONTROLS
--------
  Left stick          walk
  
  Right trigger       interact
  
  Right grip (hold)   the laser comes out
  
  Right grip (click)  the secondary button
  
  Left grip (click)   the game's "Use" action
  
  Left stick click    sprint
  
  Left trigger        duck / crouch
  
  Right stick         turn (snap\smooth turn)
  Mouse wheel         the right stick's up/down for the game's menus
                      (scrolling down = the stick up)
                      
  Right stick click   re-centre the view (**do at start of game**)
  
  Both grips (hold)   the VR settings menu

 - Flashlight: hold the LEFT GRIP and press Y to switch the mod's flashlight
   (in your left hand, pointing where the controller points) on / off.
   
 - Hold the LEFT GRIP: the right stick moves the mouse (and doesn't turn you
   or scroll the inventory while you hold it).
   
 - X + Y pause: 
 - Retro pixels can be removed: Graphics/RemoveRetroPixels (on; also in the VR
   settings menu). It switches off the game's screen effects that look like a
   pixel / retro filter, and a low render scale is set back to 1.
   
- Inventory by the left hand / prompts by the right hand: found it.

Disable: to disable Mod rename winhttp.dll to winhttp.dll.bak
